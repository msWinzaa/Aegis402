AEGIS402 · v1.0 · SECURITY FOR AUTONOMOUS x402 PAYMENTS

Payment validity is not payment security.

Autonomous clients can produce valid payment evidence while the request, resource, freshness, policy or settlement context is wrong. Aegis inserts a deterministic security boundary before resource release.

not simply “is the payment cryptographically valid?”

EXECUTION

Settlement failure
