Security between the agent and the payment.

Autonomous software can initiate HTTP-native payments, but payment intent can be substituted, replayed, manipulated, or routed to an unsafe destination. Aegis places a deterministic, fail-closed security decision boundary around the payment while distributed threat intelligence and bounded AI contribute evidence.

WHY THIS EXISTS

01 · Problem

x402 makes machine payments practical, but an autonomous payer still needs protection against replay, substitution, stale authorisation, excessive spend, hostile payment context, invalid signatures, settlement failures and concurrency abuse.

02 · Proposed solution

Aegis binds payment intent to request/resource/merchant context, validates freshness and authorisation, enforces deterministic policy, incorporates distributed evidence, and makes an explicit ALLOW / HOLD / BLOCK decision before settlement.

03 · Security philosophy

Zero trust. Payment validity alone is insufficient. Defence in depth. Multiple independent controls protect the path. Fail closed. Critical uncertainty does not silently become authorisation.

IMPLEMENTED SECURITY CAPABILITIES · v1.0
01

Request & Context Binding

Binds payment authorisation to request, resource/endpoint, merchant, context and authorised parameters.

02

Deterministic Security Core

Hard checks cover request validity, authorisation, context, nonce, freshness, spending, execution and settlement conditions.

03

Replay & Freshness

Nonce/freshness controls prevent reuse and revalidate relevant requirements before settlement.

04

Atomicity & Idempotency

Concurrent reuse of one payment/nonce is constrained to one valid execution path.

05

Policy Enforcement

Permitted amounts, resources, execution conditions, permissions and merchant/payment constraints are deterministic.

06

Execution & Cost Guard

Execution constraints and excessive cost/resource or gas conditions can stop an otherwise valid payment.

07

Settlement Integrity

Settlement is treated as a separate boundary and checked against the authorised payment context.

08

Independent Settlement Verification

EVM state can be inspected independently instead of treating facilitator responses as unquestionable truth.

09

Deterministic Risk Assessment

Security-relevant characteristics produce risk evidence without replacing the deterministic decision core.

10

Bounded AI Risk Architecture

AI may provide score, confidence and factors, bounded by deterministic risk, thresholds, policy and enforcement mode.

11

Risk Enforcement Modes

ADVISORY → MONITORED → HOLD → BLOCK HIGH-RISK → FULL ENFORCEMENT supports progressive deployment.

12

Fail-Closed Behaviour

Security-critical uncertainty becomes ALLOW only when adequately verified; otherwise HOLD/REVIEW or BLOCK applies.

13

Capability & Degraded-State Reporting

Configured, unavailable, advisory, enforced and externally dependent capabilities are distinguished.

14

Production Configuration Guards

Required production dependencies can cause startup failure rather than insecure silent fallback.

15

Administrator Authorisation

Sensitive policy, configuration, key, threat, peer, anchoring and review operations have an authorisation boundary.

16

Hash-Chained Threat Ledger

Append-only local audit events reference the previous event and carry SHA-256-derived event hashes.

17

Process-Restart Continuity

Existing ledger state is resumed so previous_hash continuity survives process restart.

18

Cryptographic Audit Verification

Previous-hash continuity, event hashes, ordering and chain integrity can be independently recomputed.

19

Security Decision Evidence

Allowed, blocked, suspicious, failed and risk-related decisions produce auditable events.

20

Demonstrable Attack Scenarios

Normal payment, substitution, replay, stale requirements, expiry, network, signature, cost, settlement and race cases are exposed.

FEASIBILITY · VIABILITY · IMPACT

Feasibility & viability

The architecture is decomposed into local security controls, explicit x402 integration, local audit state, authenticated peer intelligence and independently inspectable settlement verification. The design is demonstrable without requiring an AI service for every deterministic security check.

Impact & benefits

Aegis makes autonomous payment behaviour governable: security decisions are explainable, automation is bounded, threats can be shared, humans can approve uncertain payments, and audit evidence can be independently checked.
