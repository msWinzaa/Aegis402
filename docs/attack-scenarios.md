# Attack Scenarios

The test suite covers payment substitution, replay, stale requirements, invalid signatures, amount and settlement mismatch, peer forgery, report modification/replay/expiry, reputation poisoning and bounded Sybil influence.

Distributed scenarios include malicious proposers, forged votes, conflicting digests, duplicate messages, partitioned peers and recovery of authenticated nodes.

The repository does not claim perfect Sybil resistance or Byzantine safety outside the stated PBFT membership threshold.
