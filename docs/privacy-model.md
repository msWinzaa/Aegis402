# Privacy Model

Aegis separates payment identity, node identity and reputation subjects where possible. Reports should contain only security-relevant evidence references and indicators.

Keyed pseudonyms and scoped identifiers reduce unnecessary direct identifier reuse. They are not differential privacy, MPC, zero knowledge or anonymous reputation.

Sensitive local state can be protected through deployment encryption and access controls. Retention is configurable at the deployment layer.

Residual risks include traffic analysis, metadata correlation and compromise of the local device.
